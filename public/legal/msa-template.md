# Master Service Agreement (MSA) — Draft Template

**NEO STUDIO SPACE** · Public draft for review · Not binding until signed

---

## What this is

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is the base contract between **NEO STUDIO SPACE** ("Studio") and **you** ("Client"). Each project also has a **Statement of Work (SOW)** — usually a written package summary from our Pricing page plus anything we agree in the brief.

Nothing here is legally binding until **both sides sign** this Agreement and an SOW for the specific project.

---

## 1. Services

Studio builds **custom websites, bots, and related digital products** as described in the SOW. Work is delivered in writing — brief, scope confirmation, staging link, launch, and handoff.

Typical packages (see neostudio.space/pricing):

- Landing pages, multi-page sites, stores, bots, and add-ons
- Fixed "from" prices or agreed totals in the SOW
- Timelines estimated in the SOW (e.g. 1–2 weeks for a clear landing)

Studio does **not** use off-the-shelf templates sold as custom work. Scope outside the SOW requires a **written change** before we build it.

---

## 2. How we work

1. **Brief** — Client fills the site brief or sends equivalent details in writing.  
2. **Scope lock** — Studio confirms package, price, timeline, and deliverables in writing. Client approves before work starts.  
3. **Build** — Studio provides a **staging URL** early. Feedback in writing (Telegram or email).  
4. **Launch** — Deploy to Client hosting (or agreed host). Git access and short handoff notes.  
5. **Support** — **Two (2) weeks** of launch support included unless the SOW says otherwise.

Optional **Ongoing project care** ($350/month) can be added later for small fixes and content updates on the live project.

---

## 3. Fees & payment

- Fees are stated in the SOW (package total or milestone schedule).  
- Invoices are due within **15 days** unless the SOW says otherwise.  
- Studio may pause work after written notice if payment is overdue by more than **5 business days**.  
- **AI/API usage** on bots is billed to Client's own provider account unless the SOW explicitly includes prepaid usage.

---

## 4. Changes

Client may request changes anytime. Studio will reply in writing with impact on **price and timeline**. Work outside the current SOW starts only after Client approves the change in writing.

---

## 5. Intellectual property

When Client has **paid in full** for a deliverable (or milestone) under the SOW:

- Client owns the **custom work product** for that scope — source code, designs produced for the project, and configs created for Client.  
- Studio assigns those rights to Client upon payment.

Studio keeps:

- Pre-existing tools, internal libraries, and general know-how  
- The right to show the work in portfolio **only with Client's written OK** (Client may refuse without affecting delivery)

Studio will not lock Client into proprietary runtimes that prevent moving the project elsewhere.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

If the project involves sensitive business data, unreleased products, or personal data:

- Both sides may sign a **mutual NDA** before that material is shared.  
- Studio treats Client materials as confidential even before a formal NDA when marked as such in writing.

Staging may run on Studio infrastructure; production should run on **Client's hosting** unless the SOW says otherwise.

---

## 7. Client responsibilities

Client will:

- Provide content, access, and decisions in time for the agreed timeline  
- Review staging and send **clear, written feedback**  
- Provide hosting/domain access for launch when applicable  
- Ensure provided materials do not infringe third-party rights

Delays caused by missing Client input may shift the timeline — Studio will say so in writing.

---

## 8. Acceptance

A deliverable is accepted when Client confirms in writing **or** when Client uses it in production without listing specific defects within **5 business days** after Studio requests review.

If something does not match the written SOW scope, Client lists concrete issues in writing. Studio fixes valid scope gaps within the project timeline or as a change order if the gap is new scope.

---

## 9. Handoff

At launch (or project end), Studio provides:

- Git repository access (or export agreed in SOW)  
- Deploy notes and environment variable list (secrets redacted)  
- Short runbook: how to update content, redeploy, and who to contact

Goal: Client can maintain or hire another developer without guessing.

---

## 10. Warranty & liability

Studio warrants work will be performed professionally and match the **written SOW** at acceptance.

**After acceptance**, deliverables are provided **as-is** except for included launch support or an active care plan.

Except for confidentiality, IP breach, or willful misconduct, each party's total liability is limited to **fees paid under the active SOW in the 12 months before the claim**. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages (lost profit, etc.).

---

## 11. Term & termination

Either party may end the engagement for material breach if the other party does not fix it within **30 days** of written notice.

If the project stops:

- Client pays for completed and in-progress work per the SOW wind-down terms  
- Studio delivers paid work product and repo access for paid scope

Sections on confidentiality, IP (for paid work), and liability survive termination.

---

## 12. General

- **Governing law / disputes:** specified in the executed SOW (default: discuss in good faith first, then arbitration if needed).  
- **Entire agreement:** this MSA + signed SOW + written change orders replace informal chat.  
- **Not legal advice:** this public draft helps you review terms early — use your own lawyer before signing.
